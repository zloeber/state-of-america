# THE STATE OF AMERICA

## Deep Research, Historical Analysis, Evidence Graph & Interactive Publication

Conduct a comprehensive, multidisciplinary, data-driven investigation into the **historical and current health of the United States**, examining the relationship between:

- Economic health
- National wealth
- Wealth inequality
- Middle-class prosperity
- National debt
- Fiscal sustainability
- Financial bubbles and crises
- Population health
- Freedom and civil liberties
- Democracy and institutional stability
- Social stability
- Political polarization
- Republican and Democratic political control

The ultimate objective is to determine, as honestly as the available evidence permits:

> **When has America been healthy, wealthy, free, stable, and broadly prosperous—and when has it not—and what relationship, if any, does political control have with those outcomes?**

This is explicitly **not** a request for politically balanced rhetoric.

It is a request for **empirical honesty**.

If the evidence favors Republicans on a particular dimension, say so.

If it favors Democrats, say so.

If both parties have contributed to a problem, say so.

If neither party meaningfully explains the outcome, say so.

If a common partisan narrative is contradicted by the evidence, explicitly identify that.

If the evidence is inconclusive, say so.

Do not manufacture symmetry for the sake of appearing neutral.

Do not manufacture partisan conclusions for the sake of appearing provocative.

**Optimize for truth, methodological rigor, transparency, and falsifiability.**
