The honest summary

America is at its wealthiest ever and healthiest by most material measures (record income, record wealth, record life expectancy, crime near 75-year lows) — and simultaneously the most unequal since the Gilded Age, the most indebted in peacetime history, more financially stretched than at any point since 2008, and measurably less democratic than at any point in 60 years. No single party created this; neither party is currently fixing it. (See report Part 19 — Final Answer)
